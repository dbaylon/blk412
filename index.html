Crafted with
Purpose & Passion

BLK 412 was born from a simple belief: coffee and tea are more than beverages—they're rituals, moments of pause, and catalysts for connection.

Since 2019, we've been sourcing the finest single-origin coffee beans and premium tea leaves from sustainable farms, roasting and preparing them in small batches, and serving them alongside handcrafted pastries made fresh each morning.

Our baristas are trained in the art of espresso extraction, pour-over technique, and latte art. Every cup is crafted with intention and care.

This is more than a coffee shop—it's a gathering place where creativity flows and community thrives.
